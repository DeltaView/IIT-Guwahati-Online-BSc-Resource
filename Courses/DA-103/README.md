# DA-103 Introduction to Statistics

## Meta

- [Coursera page](https://www.coursera.org/learn/da-103-introduction-to-statistics/home/welcome)
- Pre-requisite: None

## Syllabus

Basics of probability and random variables, distribution functions, probability mass and density functions, functions of random variables, standard univariate discrete and continuous distributions; Mathematical expectations, moments, moment generating functions, inequalities; Two dimensional random variables, joint, marginal and conditional distributions, conditional expectation, independence, covariance, correlation; Law of large numbers, Central limit theorem.
